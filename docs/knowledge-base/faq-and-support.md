# Customer Support, FAQs, and Store Policies

## Customer Support Hours and Escalation Channels
- **Live Support Availability:** Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM EST. Saturday and Sunday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST.
- **Support Channels:**
  - **Email Support:** `support@example.com` (Typical response time: within 2 hours during operational hours).
  - **Phone Support:** 1-800-555-0199 for urgent shipping delays, order cancellations, or payment authorization inquiries.
  - **Human Escalation Protocol:** When interacting with Roco AI Assistant, customers can request a human agent at any time by saying "Connect me with a human agent". An expedited support ticket is automatically created in the queue.

## Payment Methods & Order Security
- We accept all major credit and debit cards: Visa, MasterCard, American Express, and Discover.
- Digital wallet checkouts: Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Shop Pay are fully integrated.
- PayPal Express Checkout is supported for verified accounts.
- We do not accept cash on delivery (COD), personal checks, or money orders.
- All transactions are encrypted via 256-bit TLS encryption and processed through PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant gateways.

## Bulk Discounts and Corporate Inquiries
- **Volume Tier:** Orders of 15 units or more of a single product line qualify for a 15% wholesale discount.
- **Custom Branding:** Corporate logo engraving or embroidery is available on HydroFlow Bottles and Nomad Canvas Daypacks for bulk orders exceeding 50 units.
- Please email `corporate@example.com` with desired quantities and delivery timelines for custom quotes.
