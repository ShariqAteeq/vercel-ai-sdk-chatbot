# Returns, Exchanges, and Refund Policy

## 30-Day Return Window
We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. We offer a 30-day return policy for most items purchased through our online store:
- The 30-day window begins on the exact delivery date confirmed by the tracking carrier.
- Items must be returned in their original, unused condition, with all original tags, packaging, and accessories intact.
- Items showing signs of wear, washing, alteration, or intentional damage are strictly non-refundable and will be returned to the sender.

## Return Shipping Fees & Process
- **Domestic Returns:** A flat return shipping and restocking fee of $5.99 is deducted from the final refund amount when using our prepaid return shipping label.
- **Defective or Damaged Products:** If an item arrived damaged, defective, or incorrect due to a fulfillment error, return shipping is 100% free, and a full refund or immediate replacement will be issued.
- **Initiating a Return:** Customers can start a return through the self-service Returns Portal or by emailing `support@example.com` with their Order ID and the reason for return.

## Refund Timelines and Payment Methods
- Once our warehouse receives and inspects the returned merchandise (typically 2 to 3 business days following package delivery), an email notification will be sent.
- Approved refunds are credited directly back to the original method of payment (credit card, PayPal, debit).
- Please allow 5 to 7 business days for the credit to appear on your bank statement, depending on your financial institution's processing cycles.
- Shipping fees paid on the initial order (e.g. Expedited or Overnight fees) are non-refundable.

## Non-Returnable & Final Sale Items
- Gift cards and digital vouchers are non-refundable.
- Swimwear and undergarments (such as the Summer Waves Swim Trunks) must have the original hygiene sticker intact; opened or worn hygiene seals cannot be returned.
- Final clearance items discounted by 50% or more are marked as "Final Sale" and cannot be returned or exchanged.
