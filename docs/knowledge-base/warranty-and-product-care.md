# Warranty and Product Care Guidelines

## One-Year Limited Manufacturer Warranty
All hardware, electronics, and technical gear (including AeroSport Wireless Earbuds, Polaris Polarized Sunglasses, and Summit Trail Hiking Boots) are backed by a comprehensive One-Year Limited Manufacturer Warranty:
- **What is covered:** Manufacturing flaws, material failures, structural stitching defects, electronic driver malfunction, and battery holding issues under normal operational use.
- **What is NOT covered:** Accidental drops, immersion beyond rated IPX resistance, improper machine laundering, unauthorized repair attempts, or normal aesthetic wear and tear.
- **Warranty Claim Submission:** Submit warranty claims via email to `warranty@example.com` with original purchase receipt, serial/batch number, and 2 clear photos of the defect.

## Product Care: AirBreeze Linen Shirt & Apparel
- **Washing:** Hand wash or machine wash on gentle cycle using cold water (30°C / 86°F max) with mild, bleach-free detergent.
- **Drying:** Air dry flat on a clean surface or line dry in the shade. Do not tumble dry, as high heat can shrink linen fibers by up to 5%.
- **Ironing:** Iron while fabric is slightly damp using the medium-high linen setting, or steam lightly to relax natural linen creases.

## Product Care: HydroFlow Insulated Water Bottle
- **Cleaning:** Hand wash daily with warm soapy water and a bottle brush. Rinse thoroughly.
- **Dishwasher Safe:** The stainless steel body is top-rack dishwasher safe, but hand washing preserves the powder-coated exterior finish longer.
- **Gasket Maintenance:** Remove the silicone lid seal once a month to wash and inspect for residue. Ensure the gasket is seated flat before tightening the cap to maintain the 24-hour vacuum seal.
- **Prohibitions:** Do not freeze the bottle, do not place in a microwave, and do not use with carbonated beverages under extreme heat.

## Product Care: Summit Trail Hiking Boots
- **Cleaning:** Brush off caked mud and dirt with a soft-bristle nylon brush after each hike. Wipe surfaces with a damp cloth.
- **Waterproofing Reapplication:** Apply a silicone-based fluoropolymer water repellent spray every 6 months to maintain the waterproof membrane's breathability and water beading performance.
