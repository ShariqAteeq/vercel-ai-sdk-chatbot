# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Domestic Shipping Options & Rates
We offer three shipping tiers for domestic deliveries within the continental United States:
- **Standard Ground Shipping:** Delivery within 3 to 5 business days. Flat rate of $4.95 for orders under $75. Free standard shipping applies automatically to all domestic orders with a subtotal of $75.00 or higher (before taxes).
- **Expedited Priority Shipping:** Delivery within 2 business days. Flat rate of $12.50. Orders placed before 1:00 PM EST Monday through Friday are processed and dispatched on the same business day.
- **Overnight Express:** Guaranteed next-business-day delivery by 3:00 PM local time. Flat rate of $24.99. Orders must be placed prior to 11:00 AM EST to qualify for same-day dispatch.

## Processing and Dispatch Windows
- All warehouse orders are processed Monday through Friday, excluding national federal holidays.
- Orders placed on Saturday or Sunday will be queued and processed the following Monday morning.
- Warehouse fulfillment typically takes between 12 to 24 hours from payment confirmation to carrier scan.

## International Shipping
- We currently ship to Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, and select European Union member countries.
- International standard transit time ranges from 7 to 14 business days depending on customs clearance.
- International shipping rates start at $18.00 and vary by destination country and total package weight.
- **Customs & Import Duties:** International customers are responsible for any applicable local customs duties, VAT, or import tariffs levied by the destination country upon arrival.

## Tracking & Carrier Partners
- All shipments are handled by our trusted logistics partners: UPS, FedEx, and USPS Priority Mail.
- As soon as your order is packaged and assigned a carrier shipping label, an automated tracking email is sent containing your tracking number and direct tracking link.
- If tracking shows "Delivered" but you have not received your package, please check with neighbors or building reception, and allow 24 hours for carrier delivery updates before contacting support.
